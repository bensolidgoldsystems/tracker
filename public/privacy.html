SGS Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

SGS Tracker is an internal tool operated by Solid Gold Systems LLC, 356 New York Ave, Apt. 8, Huntington, NY 11743. It is used only by Solid Gold Systems staff and is not offered to the public or to other businesses.

What we access

When a Solid Gold Systems team member connects our own QuickBooks Online company, SGS Tracker reads accounting reports (such as Profit & Loss) to show them in our internal reporting. SGS Tracker does not change, create or delete anything in QuickBooks.

How it is stored

Report data and connection tokens are stored in our private database (Supabase, United States), protected by sign-in with two-factor verification. Tokens are only readable by our server, never by a browser.

Sharing

We do not sell or share this data with anyone. It is used only for Solid Gold Systems' own bookkeeping and reporting.

Disconnecting

QuickBooks can be disconnected at any time from SGS Tracker Settings or from QuickBooks' connected apps. Stored report data is deleted on request.
